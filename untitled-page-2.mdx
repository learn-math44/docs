Creating a bingo game only takes a few steps.

Step 1<br />Click the "Create Math Bingo" button the homescreen. 

<Frame>
  ![Homescreen](/images/bingo44/homescreen.png "Homescreen")
</Frame>

Step 2<br />Enter a title, grade level, topic, grid size, student cards, and anwser type.

<Frame>
  ![Create Board Screen](/images/bingo44/create-board-screen.png "Create Board Screen")
</Frame>

Step 3<br />Add questions or use pre made ones.

Step 4<br />Click "Create bingo crads".

<Warning>
  Since its a no login tool every bingo card is saved onto the device.
</Warning>

Bingo44 will automatically randomize every card so students receive different layouts.

After generation you can preview, print, or save your game.
