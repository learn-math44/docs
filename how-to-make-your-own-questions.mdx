Go to your dashboard. ( [https://app.math44.org](https://app.math44.org) ).

<Frame>
  ![Teacher Dashboard Empty](/images/teacher-dashboard-empty.png)
</Frame>

On the left sidebar you will see "Question Bank". Click it.

<Frame>
  ![Question Bank Page Blank](/images/question-bank-page-blank.avif)
</Frame>

On the middle of your screen click "Create Your First Question".

<Frame>
  ![Question Maker](/images/question-maker.avif)
</Frame>

On this menu you can make your question. Onced filled out you can use it on any assignment!
