---
title: "Add Students to your Class"
icon: "plus"
---

Tell your students to go to "[app.math44.org](https://app.math44.org)". They will sign up with the prompted screen. Once signed in tell your students to click the button "Join New Class".

<Frame>
  ![Student Dashboard](/images/student-dashboard.png)
</Frame>

Your students will be prompted for that unique class code you got earlier. Your students will need to type in the class code you gave them. After they typed the code in press "Join Class".

<Frame>
  ![Enter Class Code](/images/enter-class-code.avif)
</Frame>

Your students will now be in your class! Once you assign an assessment your students will see it on their dashboard.
