---
title: "Printing Bingo Cards"
icon: "print"
---

Head to the "My games" tab at the to right of your screen.

<Frame>
  ![Bing Games](/images/bingo44/bing-games.png "Bing Games")
</Frame>

Then press the printer icon next to the bingo set you want to print.

<Frame>
  ![Print Screen](/images/bingo44/print-screen.png "Print Screen")
</Frame>

Once pressed you will be on the printing screen. On the top bar you can edit the printing styles. Press print or download as a PDF file.

<Note>
  Changing the card amount to 4 per page is recomended.
</Note>

Once your press the green "Print" button you will be presented with the printer settings.
