To create your first assignment go to your dashboard ([https://app.math44.org](https://app.math44.org)) then click "Create Your First Assignment".

<Frame>
  ![Teacher Dashboard Clean](/images/teacher-dashboard-clean.avif)
</Frame>

Once pressed you will automatically be redirected to the question word bank. This is where you can pick and chose your assignment questions!

<Frame>
  ![Assignment Hub Clear](/images/assignment-hub-clear.png)
</Frame>

As of this was published it's a shared question bank. You can click the questions you want. Then on the left hand side of the screen you will see a panel called "Create New Assignment". This panel is where you can add all the assignment information. You will give it a name and assign into your desired class. Next click "Create Assignment". You can click student progress on the sidebar to the left. This is where you can view all of your student's progress from the second they start to the second they end.

</Frame>
