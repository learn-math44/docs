### **Signing up**

Math44 offers 4 ways to create an account:

- Google Sign in
- Microsoft Sign in
- Apple Sign in
- Email and Password

Sign in using one of the options above.

<Frame>
  ![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)
</Frame>

Onced you have signed in you will be prompted to chose a role. Make sure it fits your role.

<Frame>
  ![Role Selection](/images/role-selection.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  If you ever need to change an account type, please contact support.
</Note>

For students, home educators, and home students they will be automaticly be directed to there dashboard. For teachers they will have an onboarding proceess.

Teacher's have a few questions before they will have access to there correct dashboard. Fill out the questions accordingly.

<Frame>
  ![Teacher Onboarding](/images/teacher-onboarding.png)
</Frame>

Once you have finished the teacher onboarding proccess you will be directed to the teacher dashboard.
